# Sources — Monday 23 November 2026

Every story drawn on the front page happened, or was first reported,
on Monday 23 November 2026. Each one was checked against at least
two outlets. Events from 21–22 November are listed under "Not used"

## Lead — Ukraine says it sank a Russian warship off Libya

- **Reuters**, 23 Nov 2026 — "Zelenskiy says Ukraine sank Russian
  corvette near Libya's coast, hit cargo ship": President Volodymyr
  Zelenskyy said on Monday that Ukrainian forces sank the Russian
  corvette *Merkury* (commissioned 2023) near Libya using seven
  Ukrainian-made naval drones; a Ka-27 helicopter was destroyed and
  the dry cargo ship MV *Angara* was hit.
- **Al Jazeera**, 23 Nov 2026 — "Zelenskyy says Ukraine was behind drone
  attacks on Russian ships off Libya": the attack took place on Sunday;
  Zelenskyy confirmed it on Monday; the operation was about 2,400 km
  from the front line.
- **CNN**, 23 Nov 2026 — "Ukrainian drones sink Russian warship in
  North Africa": same account; the *Merkury* was docked at Benghazi
  to refuel.

## Space / Security — NATO warns of a nuclear-armed satellite

- **Reuters**, 23 Nov 2026 — "NATO believes Russia is ready to launch
  nuclear-armed satellite, German media say": NATO members' intelligence
  services concluded Russia plans to launch a satellite armed with a
  nuclear weapon this year, NDR/WDR/Süddeutsche Zeitung reported on
  Monday; the satellite, named *Sputnik-S*, was assembled years ago
  and is stationed in the Moscow region.
- **Kyiv Post / Ukrainska Pravda**, 23 Nov 2026 — same investigation;
  a launch could come as early as December; the Kremlin dismissed the
  report on Monday.

- **Yonhap**, 23 Nov 2026 — "Lee names new FM, 3 other ministers, spy

## China — Beijing sets 2030 care targets

- **Reuters**, 23 Nov 2026 — "China sets 2030 targets to boost support
  for older people, childcare": a government blueprint published on

  renovated; under-3 childcare enrolment up 6 points from 2025.
- **Xinhua**, 23 Nov 2026 — "China rolls out plan to improve eldercare,
